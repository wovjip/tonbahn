    How to play

    1. Tap the centre (or press the space bar) to start the train.
    2. Tap a style to get a new groove in that style.
    3. Tap a ring to place a note. Towards the inside is lower, towards the outside is higher. Tap it again to remove it.
    4. Drag a melody or bass note in or out to change its pitch.
    5. Watch the style meter: move one kick and see where your groove drifts.
    6. Lower left of the circle: tap M, B or D to mute melody, bass or drums.
    7. Lower right: slide along the small arc for the Psychedelic View. It changes only the picture, not the sound.
    8. Sound pad: drag to shape the sound of the whole loop.
    9. Above the circle you see the name of your groove. The number goes up when you change it after saving.
    10. Save groove keeps it in this browser. Share link copies a link anyone can open to hear your groove.
